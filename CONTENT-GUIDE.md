# Content Migration Guide

Use this file as the central place to replace the demo content with your company information.

## Company Details
Update the values in js/content.js:
- company.name
- company.tagline
- company.description
- company.email
- company.phone
- company.address
- company.social.*

## Hero Section
- hero.badge
- hero.title
- hero.accent
- hero.subtitle

## Services

- Each service includes title, text, and icon

## Portfolio / Projects
